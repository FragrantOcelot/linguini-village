Linguini Village privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

Linguini Village is a game for learning languages. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. In short: you can play without an account, and then your progress stays on your device. If you choose to sign in, we keep a copy of your progress in the cloud so you can carry on from any device. If you subscribe to Premium in the Android app, Google Play takes the payment, and a service called RevenueCat keeps track of whether your subscription is active.

Playing without signing in

Signing in is optional. Without it, your progress stays on your device, and we don't collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or anything else that says who you are. The game has no ads and no tracking, and we don't sell anything about you.

Counting visits (web version)

The web version uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits to its pages. It uses no cookies and doesn't identify you; Cloudflare's own privacy policy covers it.

What stays on your device

To keep your place in the game, it saves your progress on your device: the language you're learning, the lessons you've passed, when each one is due for review, your test results, your XP and level, your in-game name and character, and settings such as whether the music is on. This is kept in the game's own storage on your device.

To delete it, clear the app's storage in your device's settings (or your browser's site data for the web version), or uninstall the app.

If you sign in (cloud save)

In Settings, under Cloud save, you can sign in with your Google account to keep your progress safe and play on more than one device. If you do:

  • From Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture link, as Google shares them when you sign in. We use your email address to show which account you're signed in with. We don't use your name or picture.
  • Your progress (the same as listed above) is copied to our database whenever it changes, and copied back to any device you sign in on.
  • Like most online services, the servers that handle sign-in and saving record technical details such as your IP address and the time of each request, to keep the service secure and working.

We use this only to run cloud save. We don't sell it, don't use it for advertising, and don't share it with anyone except the services that run it for us:

  • Supabase (supabase.com) stores your account and your progress, and handles signing in.
  • Google confirms who you are when you sign in with Google. Google's own privacy policy covers what it does.

Your information is sent over encrypted connections. Signing out removes it from that device only; your cloud copy stays until you delete your account.

Premium (the subscription, Android app)

Some places and extra languages come with Premium, a monthly subscription you can buy in the Android app. If you look at it or buy it:

  • Google Play takes the payment and handles the subscription: renewing, cancelling and refunds. We never see your card or payment details. Google's own privacy policy covers what it does.
  • RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) checks with Google Play whether your subscription is active, so the game can open what it includes, on every device you use. To do that, the app gives RevenueCat an ID: a random one it makes for your copy of the game, or your cloud save account's ID if you've signed in (never your name or email address). RevenueCat receives your purchase history in the game (what you bought, when, the price and currency) and basic technical details such as the app's version, your device's system version, language and country, and, like most online services, your IP address.

We use this only to run the subscription and to see how it's doing (for example how many people subscribe). We don't sell it or use it for advertising. RevenueCat works for us as a service provider. To cancel, go to Google Play, then Subscriptions.

Deleting your account

In the game, go to Settings, then Cloud save, then Delete account. Your account and your cloud progress are deleted straight away, for good. The progress on the device you're using stays on it. You can also email us (below) to ask us to delete your account, or your purchase records at RevenueCat. Google Play keeps its own record of your purchases, under Google's policy.

Internet access

The game works offline. It only uses the internet to sign in and keep your cloud save up to date (if you've signed in), and, in the Android app, to check and buy Premium and to get updates.

Children

The game is for everyone and can be played without an account. Signing in uses a Google account; if you're under 13, or under the age of digital consent where you live, please ask a parent or guardian before signing in. If you believe a child has signed in without a parent's permission, contact us and we'll delete the account.
