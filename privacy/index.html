Linguini Village privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Linguini Village is a game for learning languages. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. In short: you can play without an account, and then everything stays on your device. If you choose to sign in, we keep a copy of your progress in the cloud so you can carry on from any device, and nothing else.

Playing without signing in

Signing in is optional. Without it, the game collects nothing: no name, email address, location, contacts, photos or anything else. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing about you is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

To keep your place in the game, it saves your progress on your device: the language you're learning, the lessons you've passed, when each one is due for review, your test results, your XP and level, your in-game name and character, and settings such as whether the music is on. This is kept in the game's own storage on your device.

To delete it, clear the app's storage in your device's settings (or your browser's site data for the web version), or uninstall the app.

If you sign in (cloud save)

In Settings, under Cloud save, you can sign in with your Google account to keep your progress safe and play on more than one device. If you do:

  • From Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture link, as Google shares them when you sign in. We use your email address to show which account you're signed in with. We don't use your name or picture.
  • Your progress (the same as listed above) is copied to our database whenever it changes, and copied back to any device you sign in on.
  • Like most online services, the servers that handle sign-in and saving record technical details such as your IP address and the time of each request, to keep the service secure and working.

We use this only to run cloud save. We don't sell it, don't use it for advertising, and don't share it with anyone except the services that run it for us:

  • Supabase (supabase.com) stores your account and your progress, and handles signing in.
  • Google confirms who you are when you sign in with Google. Google's own privacy policy covers what it does.

Your information is sent over encrypted connections. Signing out removes it from that device only; your cloud copy stays until you delete your account.

Deleting your account

In the game, go to Settings, then Cloud save, then Delete account. Your account and your cloud progress are deleted straight away, for good. The progress on the device you're using stays on it. You can also email us (below) to ask us to delete your account.

Internet access

The game works offline. It only uses the internet to sign in and to keep your cloud save up to date (if you've signed in), and, in the Android app, so Google Play can offer updates.

Children

The game is for everyone and can be played without an account. Signing in uses a Google account; if you're under 13, or under the age of digital consent where you live, please ask a parent or guardian before signing in. If you believe a child has signed in without a parent's permission, contact us and we'll delete the account.
