Linguini Village privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

Linguini Village is a game for learning Emirati Arabic. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. In short: the game collects nothing, and everything stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Linguini Village has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It doesn't ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it doesn't send anything about you to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

To keep your place in the game, the app saves your progress on your device: the lessons you've passed, when each one is due for review, your test results, your XP and level, and settings such as whether the music is on. This is stored only in the app's own storage on your device. It is never uploaded, and we can't see it.

To delete it, clear the app's storage in your device's settings, or uninstall the app.

Internet access

The app is built with a framework that includes the internet permission, but Linguini Village doesn't use it to send or receive any data. Everything in the game is already on your device, so it works offline.

Children

The game doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
